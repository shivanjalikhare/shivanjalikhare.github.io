Artistic Perspective

My journey in spatial design began with observational sketching and watercolor painting. Hand drawing allows me to feel the proportions, structural weight, and natural light before turning to digital tools.

Sustainable Vision

Buildings account for nearly 40% of global emissions. My goal is to understand passive cooling, timber engineering, and low-embodied carbon materials to create architecture in harmony with nature.
